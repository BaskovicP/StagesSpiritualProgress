# Review against three-ways.md

## Scope

The user-referred `3-ways.md` is present as `three-ways.md`. The whole supplied text was reviewed alongside the 36 version-6 questions. Version 7 later added three questions from `Navitagint.md`; they are outside this three-ways wording review. The supplied file is preserved as received and supplies theological context, not a scoring table.

Twenty questions received clearer titles, examples or clarifications in both Croatian and English. The other sixteen were reviewed and left unchanged where they already express a concrete practice with suitable safeguards. All 36 IDs, answer options, option indices, expectations and scoring rules remain unchanged in this review. Version 6 and its saved answers therefore remain compatible. These changes clarify existing constructs, not validate them.

## Decisions grounded in the new text

| Passage | Implication for the questionnaire |
| --- | --- |
| Division of the states or ways; The purgative way | Growth involves grace and a person's response; virtue practice and examen are not a self-improvement competition. |
| The illuminative way | Recollection is compatible with duties and honest, necessary recreation. Neither devotion nor detachment requires abandoning ordinary life. |
| The illuminative way; The unitive way | Subjects such as the Passion or Resurrection can nourish prayer across the ways; the topic selected does not identify a stage. |
| Consolation; Desolation | Acts of trust, charity and the will can coexist with distress. Affective prayer does not require emotional warmth. Dryness can have different causes and cannot identify a dark night, regression or loss of grace. Relief and help may be sought. |
| The unitive way; Directions (1)–(3) | The ways overlap, purification continues, and further growth remains possible. Mystical favors are not reliable rank markers. The text specifically says the gift of contemplation is not received by every soul advanced in charity. |
| Directions (4) | Spiritual stage is not a gate for frequent Communion. The app does not adjudicate eligibility for Communion; no historical disciplinary passage has been turned into new user instructions or scoring requirements. |

## How the two sources are kept distinct

The original `stages-of-spiritual-progress.md` still supplies the I–VI descriptions and their source ceilings. Its text is not silently rewritten to make both documents appear identical.

The additional document discusses overlapping ways and predominance of charity, not a cumulative checklist. It also explicitly distinguishes growth in charity from receipt of contemplative or extraordinary gifts. Consequently, the existing strict requirement algorithm is retained only as an author-defined comparison with practical descriptions, **not a theological test that can assign a person's actual way**. The introduction, result caution and advanced-result note make that distinction explicit.

No new mystical questions, experience bonuses, confidence percentages, compulsory prayer topics, austerity requirements or sacramental eligibility rules were added. Absence of a mystical report cannot lower a score: there is no such input or hidden criterion. Pain, illness, abusive situations and necessary care are not reframed as personal failures.
